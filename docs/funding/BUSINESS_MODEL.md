## 1. Business Model Overview

WARAVUTAI OS is being developed as a software platform combining AI-assisted workflows, creator-oriented software, backend services, runtime capabilities, and evidence-aware infrastructure.

The commercial strategy is to begin with a focused customer problem and a narrow product offering rather than attempting to commercialize the entire platform simultaneously.

The initial business model will be validated through customer discovery, MVP usage, pilot deployments, willingness-to-pay testing, and measurable customer outcomes.

---

## 2. Target Customers

Potential initial customer groups include:

- Individual creators
- Freelance creators
- Small creative businesses
- Digital content teams
- Businesses seeking AI-assisted content workflows
- Organizations requiring structured AI-enabled workflows

The initial customer segment remains subject to validation.

## 3. Customer Problem

Potential customers may face challenges including:

- Time-consuming content production
- Fragmented creative and AI tools
- Repetitive workflow tasks
- Difficulty organizing AI-assisted processes
- Infrastructure complexity
- Lack of integrated creator workflows
- Difficulty moving from experimentation to repeatable production

The product will prioritize the problems demonstrated to have the strongest customer demand.

---

## 4. Value Proposition

WARAVUTAI OS aims to provide an integrated environment for AI-assisted workflows and software capabilities.

The intended value proposition includes:

- Reduced workflow complexity
- Faster execution of repetitive tasks
- AI-assisted productivity
- Structured creator workflows
- Integrated application and backend capabilities
- Evidence-aware system design
- A foundation that can evolve with validated customer demand

The value proposition will be refined through real customer usage.
