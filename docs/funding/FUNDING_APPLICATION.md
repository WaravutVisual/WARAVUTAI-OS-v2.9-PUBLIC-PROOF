## 1. Executive Summary

WARAVUTAI OS is an AI-native software platform under active development, designed to combine creator-focused software, AI workflows, device and runtime capabilities, and evidence-aware system infrastructure.

The project is moving from technical foundation development toward a focused customer-facing MVP, customer validation, revenue generation, and production deployment.

Funding support will be used to accelerate MVP development, cloud infrastructure, AI workflow services, backend development, testing, security, observability, and initial customer pilots.

The immediate objective is to transform the existing technical foundation into a focused product that can be demonstrated, tested with real users, and validated commercially.

---

## 2. Project Overview

WARAVUTAI OS is being developed as a layered software platform.

The platform includes:

- PANDA for device and runtime-oriented capabilities
- CREATOR for creator-focused SaaS and AI workflows
- AI CORE for AI workflow and model orchestration
- G16 for evidence and runtime infrastructure
- G23 for evidence validation and claim-boundary controls

The project emphasizes clear separation between implementation, evidence, validation, derived state, and external claims.

This approach is intended to support responsible development of AI-enabled software while maintaining explicit boundaries around what has and has not been verified.

## 3. Problem

Developers and businesses increasingly depend on AI-generated workflows and automated software systems.

However, moving from an AI capability or prototype to a reliable product introduces additional challenges:

- Integrating AI into usable customer workflows
- Managing backend and runtime infrastructure
- Maintaining reliable application behavior
- Handling evidence and system state
- Distinguishing verified information from unsupported claims
- Controlling infrastructure and AI operating costs
- Moving from prototype development to commercially validated products

WARAVUTAI OS is being developed to address these challenges through an integrated product and infrastructure approach.

## 4. Proposed Solution

WARAVUTAI OS combines AI workflow capabilities with application, backend, runtime, and evidence-aware infrastructure.

## 16. Risks and Mitigation

### Product Risk

The initial product may not solve a sufficiently valuable customer problem.

**Mitigation:** Use customer interviews, pilot usage, and measurable feedback to validate the product before expanding scope.

### Technical Risk

AI services, backend systems, or infrastructure may require additional engineering before reliable production operation.

**Mitigation:** Use staged development, controlled deployment, monitoring, testing, and incremental production hardening.

### Market Risk

The initial customer segment or pricing model may require adjustment.

**Mitigation:** Test multiple customer hypotheses and refine the commercial offer based on willingness to pay.

### Cost Risk

AI inference and cloud infrastructure costs may increase with usage.

**Mitigation:** Monitor usage, optimize workloads, control infrastructure growth, and prioritize validated workloads.

### Funding Risk

Available funding may be insufficient to complete all planned development.

**Mitigation:** Prioritize the smallest viable MVP and allocate resources against measurable milestones.
